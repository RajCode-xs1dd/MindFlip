About MindFlip

MindFlip is a visual sequence challenge built around attention, spatial memory, and pattern recognition.

Core Rule: All cards start face-down. You must discover the hidden sequence in the correct order.

Multiplayer: Share the device. Take turns. A mistake ends your turn. First to complete the full sequence wins immediately.
